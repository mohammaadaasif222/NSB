What each side provides

You provide

  • The existing demo frontend files
  • Brand name, logo and colours
  • 5 to 10 real projects: photos, floor plans, RERA numbers, prices by unit type, amenities
  • Cities and localities you want to show first
  • Domain name and server, or approval to buy them on your account
  • One person to review each milestone demo within 2 working days

Not included in the price

  • Server and domain: about ₹600 to ₹900 a month, paid to the provider
  • SMS or WhatsApp messages: charged per message by the provider
  • Online booking payments through a payment gateway (can be quoted separately)
  • Mobile apps for Play Store or App Store
  • New features after the scope is agreed (quoted separately)

The 12-week plan has no spare days, so it depends on reviews coming back within 2 working days and on the scope staying as listed here. If a review takes longer, the launch date moves by the same amount. Prices assume one full-stack developer at ₹1,600 per working day, 5 working days a week. This estimate is valid for 30 days.
